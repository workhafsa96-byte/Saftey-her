About SafeHer

Built for Every Woman, Every Situation

We believe safety is a right, not a privilege. SafeHer combines technology and awareness to help women feel secure at home, at work, and on the move.

Our Purpose

To give every woman instant access to safety tools — from emergency alerts to nearby help — right from her phone or laptop.

Our Mission

Reduce response time in emergencies and build a supportive network of trusted contacts, resources, and community awareness.

Why It Matters

Millions of women face safety concerns daily. Quick access to help, information, and community can make all the difference.

Self-Defense Tips

Stay Alert, Stay Safe

Simple, practical tips that can help you stay safe in everyday situations.

Stay Aware

Keep your head up and avoid distractions like your phone in unfamiliar areas.

Know Your Route

Plan your route in advance and stick to well-lit, populated streets.

Trust Your Gut

If something feels wrong, remove yourself from the situation immediately.

Stay in Groups

Travel with friends or trusted people whenever possible, especially at night.

Keep Phone Charged

Always keep your phone charged and easily accessible for emergencies.

Learn Basic Self-Defense

Consider taking a self-defense class to build confidence and preparedness.

Emergency Toolkit

Everyday Tools That Save Lives

Quick guides on essential safety tools and habits.

Flashlight Guide

Keep a flashlight app or torch handy. Flash it rapidly to signal for help in the dark.

Whistle

A loud whistle can startle an attacker and alert people nearby faster than shouting.

First Aid

Carry a small first-aid kit with antiseptic, band-aids, and any personal medication.

Power Bank Tips

Always carry a charged power bank — a dead phone means no calls, maps, or alerts.

Safe Transport

Verify ride details before boarding and share trip status with a trusted contact.

Stay Prepared

Review your safety checklist and toolkit regularly so you're always ready.
